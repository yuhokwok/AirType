### description

電腦上打字，iPhone、iPad 上接收。

回覆長訊息、整理筆記，或輸入一大段文字時，繼續使用你熟悉的電腦鍵盤。AirType 透過同一 Wi-Fi，將電腦瀏覽器內輸入的文字，傳到 iPhone 或 iPad 目前的文字欄位。

打開瀏覽器，就能開始
電腦毋須安裝額外軟件，也毋須建立帳戶。在電腦瀏覽器開啟 AirType 鍵盤顯示的網址，再輸入六位數配對碼，即可連接。

用你熟悉的輸入方式
在電腦上使用中文或其他語言輸入法，完成文字後按 Enter 或傳送按鈕。瀏覽器亦提供刪除及換行控制，並內置可搜尋的 Emoji 選單，方便把表情符號加入草稿。

手機上也能直接打字
隨時切換至本機英文 QWERTY 鍵盤，支援淺色及深色外觀、數字、符號與英文拼字建議。長按空白鍵並左右拖動，可移動游標。

連線中斷，保留未送草稿
瀏覽器遇到斷線時會嘗試重新連接，最多持續 10 秒；之後可按 Reconnect 手動再試。未送出的草稿會保留在目前開啟的網頁，方便你繼續編輯。

開始使用
1. 在系統設定加入 AirType 鍵盤，並允許「完整取用」。
2. 在 AirType App 啟用本地網絡連線，允許本地網絡存取。
3. 將電腦與 iPhone 或 iPad 連接至同一 Wi-Fi。
4. 在支援的 App 開啟文字欄位，切換至 AirType 並保持 Web Access 畫面顯示。
5. 在電腦開啟鍵盤所示網址，輸入配對碼，開始打字。

使用須知
• 需要 iOS／iPadOS 17 或以上版本。
• 遠端輸入需要「完整取用」及本地網絡權限。本機打字毋須完整取用。
• 連線只在 Web Access 鍵盤畫面顯示時運作。
• 部分 App 不支援第三方鍵盤；密碼及部分電話號碼欄位會使用系統鍵盤。
• AirType Web Access 使用本地 HTTP 連線，傳輸不加密。請使用可信任的 Wi-Fi。
• 無須雲端帳戶；AirType 不會將輸入文字上傳至自家雲端服務。網頁草稿只保留於當前頁面，重新整理或關閉頁面可能遺失草稿。

### name

AirType - Computer Keyboard

### subtitle

Type on your iPhone over Wi-Fi

### promotional_text

Use your computer keyboard to type on iPhone or iPad over the same Wi-Fi. Open a browser, enter the six-digit pairing code, and start typing. No desktop app needed.

### keywords

wifi,wireless,remote,typing,browser,text,transfer,ipad,desktop,input,emoji

### description

Type on your computer. Receive it on your iPhone or iPad.

Keep using the keyboard you know when writing a long message, taking notes, or entering a paragraph on your phone. AirType sends text from your computer browser to the active text field on your iPhone or iPad over the same Wi-Fi network.

JUST OPEN A BROWSER
No desktop software or account is required. Open the address displayed on the AirType keyboard and enter its six-digit pairing code to connect.

USE YOUR FAMILIAR INPUT METHOD
Compose text with your computer’s input method, then press Enter or click Send. The browser also offers delete and newline controls, plus a searchable emoji picker for adding emoji to your draft.

TYPE LOCALLY, TOO
Switch to the built-in English QWERTY keyboard with light and dark appearances, numbers, symbols, and English spelling suggestions. Hold the space bar and drag horizontally to move the cursor.

KEEP YOUR UNSENT DRAFT
If the connection drops, the browser attempts to reconnect for up to 10 seconds, then offers a manual Reconnect button. Unsent text stays in the current page so you can continue editing.

GET STARTED
1. Add AirType in Keyboard settings and enable Allow Full Access.
2. Open AirType and enable its local network connection. Allow Local Network access.
3. Connect your computer and iPhone or iPad to the same Wi-Fi.
4. Open a text field in a supported app, switch to AirType, and keep Web Access visible.
5. Open the keyboard’s address on your computer, enter the pairing code, and type.

PLEASE NOTE
• Requires iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
• Remote typing requires Full Access and Local Network permission. Local typing works without Full Access.
• The receiving connection runs only while the Web Access keyboard is visible.
• Some apps do not allow third-party keyboards. Secure text fields and certain phone fields use the system keyboard.
• Web Access uses unencrypted local HTTP. Use a trusted Wi-Fi network.
• No cloud account is needed. AirType does not upload your typing to its own cloud service. Browser drafts remain only in the current page and may be lost if you refresh or close it.
